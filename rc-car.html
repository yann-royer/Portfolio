RC Car

An FPV car built from spare parts, with drone brushless motors and two Arduinos talking over UART.

Constraints

Final RC FPV Car. To complete the project, I could only use the spare parts that I already had at home, ultimately resulting in a design that is not optimal.

Motors

The difficulty came from using drone brushless motors that spin at up to 23,000 rpm. I tried many designs, such as an internally driven gear rim or a caterpillar track. However, the torque of the brushless motors is really low and the friction was too high.

Electronics

The library to control the ESCs and the one to read the radio receiver both used Timer 2, which is why I used 2 microcontrollers communicating over UART.

Looking back

In the end, the car drove well, but it bounced on every bit of gravel on the road. If I built it again, I would use motors suited to the job and add shock absorbers.
