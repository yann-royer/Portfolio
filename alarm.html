Alarm Clock Enhancer

An add-on for my JBL alarm clock: when the alarm goes off, it turns on my room's light and plays music.

  • Light sensor
  • Relay
  • Jack
  • SD card
Year
2020
Duration
2 weeks
Team
Solo
Status
Used until I had to move out
CAD
SolidWorks

The idea

The goal of this project was to make waking up in the morning easier. I have a JBL Horizon 1 alarm clock. What makes it special is that it lights up when the alarm goes off, and it has a jack input to play music through its built-in speakers.

How it works

This device fits around the alarm clock. As soon as the alarm goes off, it detects the light, turns on my room's light with the relay, then plays a random song from a playlist on an SD card inside the device. The music plays through the JBL's built-in speaker.
